Product Design

Great products combine powerful technology with thoughtful design to solve meaningful problems.

I focus on creating experiences that are useful, simple, and well-crafted—while also delivering measurable outcomes such as growth, engagement, and retention. By grounding vision in research and leveraging new technologies like AI, design can shape products that are both human-centered and forward-looking.

Scareproof

Personal Project - Creator/Designer

2013

This was an original idea of mine that I created with a mobile developer. It aimed to show kids that there are no monsters or bad things in their room at night. It also acted as a nightlight for extra security.

  • Scareproof (Tablet)

    Designed with a simple UX and UI so it would be easy for kids to use.

  • Scareproof (Tablet)

    Scarespray could be "sprayed" around the room to ward off monsters.

  • Scareproof (Tablet)

    A radar would track any bad things in their room. Of course it never shows anything.

  • Scareproof (Tablet)

    I added a timer so kids could use it as a nightlight and have it turn off at a specific time.

  • Scareproof (Tablet)

    Another feature was a "scanner" that kids could use to scan their room.

  • Scareproof (Mobile)

    There was also a mobile version released on iPhone and Android.

  • Scareproof Website

    A simple website was created to show off the app and offer tee shirts for kids.
